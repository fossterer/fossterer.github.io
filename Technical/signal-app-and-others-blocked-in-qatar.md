# Signal app and others blocked in Qatar?

I am in Doha airport (Qatar - Nov 08 2026). Signal messages are not getting delivered.

I first found [this post](https://signal.org/blog/looking-back-on-the-front/) but that's from 2018.

[This post](https://support.signal.org/hc/en-us/articles/360007318111-Can-I-use-Signal-when-I-travel) suggests testing the "Note to Self" approach which does work. However, messages to individual chats as well as group chats don't.

Now, according to [this post from 2026](https://simology.io/blog/qatar-voip-roaming-rules-2026-messaging-calling-workarounds), WhatsApp calls too shouldn't work. Yet I just made a video call over WhatsApp.

Things aren't consistent.
