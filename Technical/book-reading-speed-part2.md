# Book Reading Speed - Part 2

Q1) Any techniques to speed up but never losing grasp of concepts?

A) Speed up dense technical books without losing understanding by separating “first pass” comprehension from “notes/verification,” and by using structured note-taking.

## 1) Two-pass strategy (fast first, deep second)
- **Pass 1 (fast):** skim headings, intro/conclusion of sections, and diagrams. Read at normal flow rate with minimal pausing. Mark only: (a) terms you genuinely don’t get yet, (b) models/steps you expect to use later.
- **Pass 2 (deep):** go back only to the marked parts and work them more slowly, with notes and examples.

This usually preserves conceptual grasp while cutting time spent hovering over sections you’ll understand after context.
