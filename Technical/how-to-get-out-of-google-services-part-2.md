# How to get out of Google Services - Part 2 - Still with same Google account for 3 reasons

Now, don't get the wrong idea. In a previous blog post, [How to get out of Google Services - Part 1 - Gmail, Google Drive etc.](how-to-get-out-of-google-services-part-1.md), I already established that I am keeping Google for their unmatched *Google Maps* offering.
However, at the time, I intended to delete the existing account and start afresh.

Now, there are 3 reasons I don't think that's a worthy effort.

## Reason 1 - Family member accounts using my account as their "Recovery Method"

This is not a big trouble but surely avoidable, for others' sake!

## Reason 2 - Existing Play Store purchases

It's a sure **No!** Who would have thought?

There are many arguments that just put it out plainly -

    Do any of the services out there allow you to transfer your content from one account to another?

i.e Can you buy a movie in your Amazon Prime account and then hoose to transfer at least that purchase to the Amazon Prime account of a friend?

No, that is not allowed.

## Reason 3 - Needing to add the new account in my Android phone

Sure, I could do that but it takes time.

Given reasons 1 and 2, this reason 3 becomes an avoidable cost.

By avoiding this, I can continue to keep using *Google Maps* as-is. I'll just hide all Google-specific apps such as Google Drive, Google Photos etc.

Only ones remaining would be - (1) *Google Maps* and (2) *Play Store*
